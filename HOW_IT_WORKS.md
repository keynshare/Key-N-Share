# 🚀 How KeyNShare Works: A Step-by-Step Story

Ever wondered what happens when you click a button on KeyNShare? Here are the two most important stories!

---

## 🏗️ Story 1: Uploading a New Dataset
*Imagine you are bringing a new book to the library.*

1.  **The Cover & Title (Frontend):** You type in the name and pick a picture on the website.
2.  **The Locking Box (Encryption):** Before the file leaves your computer, we put it in a "Digital Safe" so only people with a key can open it.
3.  **The Magic Receipt (Blockchain):** The website asks the **Magic Notebook** to write down: *"Owner: [Your Name], Price: 1 SOL"*.
4.  **The Library Shelf (Backend):** The website tells the **Back Office** to put the (locked) file and the receipt info on the virtual shelves so everyone can see it.

---

## 💰 Story 2: Buying a Dataset
*Imagine you are buying a secret map from a treasure hunter.*

1.  **The Selection (Frontend):** you find a dataset you like and click **"Buy"**. 
2.  **The Payment (Solana/Wallet):** You use your digital wallet (like Phantom) to send some coins to the seller.
3.  **The Handover (Blockchain):** The **Magic Notebook** sees the coins move and writes: *"New Owner: [Your Name]"*.
4.  **The Key (Backend):** Once the Back Office sees the Notebook is updated, it gives you the "Secret Key" to open the "Digital Safe" from Story 1.
5.  **Success!** You can now unlock and use your new dataset!
