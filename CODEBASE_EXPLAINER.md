# 🗺️ KeyNShare: The Big Map

Hello future helper! 👋 Welcome to **KeyNShare**. 

Imagine KeyNShare is a big, magic **Toy Store** where people can share and sell their special digital "toys" (which we call **Datasets**). 

This project is made of three big parts that work together like a team. Here is how they work:

---

## 🏠 1. The Frontend (The Shop Window)
**Located in:** `client/`

This is the part you see on your screen! It’s like the **Shop Window** and the **Store Shelves**.
- **What it does:** It shows pretty pictures of datasets, has buttons you can click, and lets you "walk around" the store.
- **Why we use it:** So people can easily find what they want without looking at boring computer code.
- **Learn more:** Check out the [Frontend Guide](./client/README_FRONTEND.md) or the [How it Works Guide](./HOW_IT_WORKS.md)

---

## ⚙️ 2. The Backend (The Back Office)
**Located in:** `server/`

This is the part hidden behind the wall. It’s like the **Back Office** and the **Storeroom**.
- **What it does:** It remembers who is logged in, keeps a list of every dataset in a big "Filing Cabinet" (Database), and makes sure everyone follows the rules.
- **Why we use it:** To keep everything organized and safe. 
- **Learn more:** Check out the [Backend Guide](./server/README_BACKEND.md)

---

## ⛓️ 3. The Blockchain (The Magic Receipt Book)
**Located in:** `dataset_metadata/`

This is the most special part! Imagine a **Magic Notebook** that stays in the middle of the town square. Anyone can look at it, but NO ONE can erase what’s written in it.
- **What it does:** Every time someone uploads a dataset, we write a "Receipt" in this magic notebook. This proves who owns it forever!
- **Why we use it:** So we don't have to just "trust" a single person. The notebook never lies!
- **Learn more:** Check out the [Blockchain Guide](./dataset_metadata/README_BLOCKCHAIN.md)

---

## 🚀 How a "Toy" (Dataset) Moves Through the Shop

1. **You Upload:** You bring your "toy" to the shop.
2. **The Shop Window (Frontend)** asks you for a name and a picture.
3. **The Magic Notebook (Blockchain)** writes down that YOU are the owner.
4. **The Back Office (Backend)** puts it on the shelf so others can see it.
5. **Someone Buys:** They pay some digital coins, the Magic Notebook writes a new receipt, and the Shop lets them take the toy home!
